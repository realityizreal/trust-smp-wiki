The testing system

How Trust SMP PvP tiers are assessed

Tier structure & point values

Each gamemode has its own tier ranking. The tier labels run from S at the top to F at the bottom. There is exactly one S-tier player per gamemode; A through F can have multiple players.

S · 30Top tier · one player per gamemode
A · 20High-level player
B · 15Strong player
C · 10Intermediate tier
D · 5Developing tier
F · 1Entry tier
These are the point values listed by Eroision. The exact formula for combining points across multiple gamemodes into an overall ranking has not been documented here, so the wiki does not assume one.

How a test works

Current documented rules

1
Choose a mode

Pick the gamemode

The player is tested in a specific gamemode. A player's tier can differ between modes, so a Sword rank does not automatically determine their Mace or MSMP rank.

2
Play the rounds

Complete the required test

The number of rounds depends on the gamemode and, for Sword, the tier being tested. The round requirements are listed below.

3
Evaluate

Assess the performance

The result matters, but the test is not simply a win/loss checkbox. Performance and how close the rounds are can matter; a close loss may still demonstrate the level needed for a tier.

4
Record

Update the tierlist

The awarded tier is recorded for that gamemode. High, mid, and low placements can further order players within the same tier.

Testers & spectators

Players of any tier can act as testers. Spectators are ideally C-tier or higher. Testers are also evaluated during the test: for example, in a 10–8 Sword test, the tester can earn the tier being tested for as well, depending on the result and assessment.

Server history

Five eras that shaped the community

👑 Golden Age Era — Meatball7006

The earliest SMP period. Meatball7006 was dominant in grinding and survival, while the PvP skill ceiling was generally lower than in later eras. Limited00000 stood out as an unusually skilled exception.

🏗 Founder Era — Steven7lo

A period of server development and community growth. Steven7lo was known as an exceptional builder, early redstone talent, and strong PvPer.

⚔ PvP Era — Fx11_n

Competitive PvP became a defining part of the server. Players increasingly took testing, improvement, and gamemode specialization seriously.

🔨 Mace Era — Abyssalsovereign

Abyssal popularized mace PvP and introduced the style to several players across the community.

🔥 Modern Era — Eroision

The skill ceiling rose through advanced utility, counters, and techniques such as stun webs and web drains.

Major events

Memorable moments from Trust SMP history

⚔
Team fight

The 3v5 End Fight

V0lDAURA_, Chubbzzz__, and boihandsomeboi faced Eroision, ImKaizer_, Niqhtmc, Emu1at0r, and echobladez_. The trio used End-edge and void tactics rather than relying on a straight melee fight. Niqhtmc was knocked into the void with a wind charge, V0lDAURA_ knocked Eroision off during the fight, and the trio eventually finished Kaizer after his armor broke.

💥
Investigation

The Trial Chamber Griefing

Chubbzzz__, boihandsomeboi, Abyssal, and Noogetman had spent hours redesigning a Trial Chamber. At around 8 PM, all five players online were suddenly teleported to Chubbzzz, then hundreds of TNT entities exploded and destroyed the build. The Shadow was initially suspected because the group had been teleported.

How Eroision investigated it: Chubbzzz's recording showed him building and not placing TNT. The speed and scale of the blast suggested a mass command such as /fill, but the logs showed no recent use of that command. Eroision then found that Abyssal had used /fill about 12 hours earlier to create and detonate thousands of TNT entities. The key discovery was that The Shadow's teleport had also moved existing TNT entities. Eroision tested teleporting TNT entities and reproduced the effect.

Conclusion: The Shadow's teleport triggered the explosion, but the TNT entities originated from Abyssal's earlier setup. The Shadow was cleared of deliberately creating the TNT for the griefing. The Trial Chamber remained destroyed.

🏆
Tier change

CrystaIized Takes Sword S

CrystaIized (Discord name Shinju) defeated ImKaizer_ 10–4 in a Sword S-tier test, earning S. Kaizer moved down to high A.

🛡
PvP history

Chubbzzz__ Cheating Incidents

Eroision caught Chubbzzz__ using banned PvP modifications/macros on multiple occasions. One specifically remembered incident involved a multi-keybind setup: a single axe input could register five shield-hit inputs. Chubbzzz demonstrated that the keybind could be increased as high as 30 clicks from one physical input.

Why it mattered: The setup compressed repeated inputs into one action, giving an unfair advantage in shield interactions. The anti-cheat detected the behavior and issued an immediate ban.

Archive status: Multiple cheating incidents have been mentioned, but this is the one with detailed mechanics currently documented. The wiki deliberately leaves the other incidents unspecified until their exact details are confirmed.

Techniques

Advanced mechanics and signature strategies

⚡
Advanced

Rapture Tech

A recognized high-level technique. The three players currently known to perform it are Drowsey2249, Abyssalsovereign, and CrystaIized.

🕸
Eroision signature

Stun Web

A utility sequence involving shield pressure, axe hits, and a cobweb placed to restrict the opponent's movement. Eroision is especially known for refining and consistently landing it.

💧
Counterplay

Web Drain

A web-based pressure tactic that can make it difficult for an opponent to escape or recover. Players have developed water placement and potion-based responses.

🏹
Cart PvP

Cart X-Bow

A TNT minecart and crossbow interaction associated with Eroision's Cart play. The wiki can expand this page with safe in-game demonstrations and match clips.
